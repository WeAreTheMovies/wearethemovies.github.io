About

We Are The Movies is a rock band from Columbus, Ohio. The group’s catalog includes Ending One Moment at a Time (2013), Get Busy Living… (2016), …Or Get Busy Dying (2018), original singles and cover recordings.

Early releases

The band released Ending One Moment at a Time in 2013. Its follow-up, Get Busy Living…, arrived in March 2016 through Broken Line Records. The six-track release included “The Story So Far,” “Temporary” and “By a Thread.”

Alaska and Road to Warped Tour

In June 2016, We Are The Movies traveled to Alaska, where vocalist Tim Waters grew up. The trip included a show at Chilkoot Charlie’s and a main-stage appearance at Road to Warped Tour in Anchorage on June 22. The band also appeared on KWHL’s Bob & Chad radio show and played an acoustic set for Covenant House Alaska. The tour was documented by musician Zach Frost, who traveled with the group.

Recordings and collaborations

…Or Get Busy Dying was released on September 21, 2018. Produced, mixed and mastered by Waters at Radio City Records, it includes “Leaving Me Incomplete,” featuring Nick Thompson of Hit the Lights. The band discussed the recording with The Spinning Thoughts and later appeared on The Sounds of Bustown podcast.

Alternative Press named We Are The Movies one of its Bands to Watch in 2020. Shortly after the magazine article, the world went into lockdown. Despite social distancing, the band continued making music, releasing covers over the following years interspersed with original singles.

“Bury Me,” featuring Oshie Bichar of Beartooth and City Lights, arrived in October 2020. “The Devil Inside” followed in 2021, with its video premiered by New Noise Magazine. Covers included “Stay” with Cotter and “Wonder,” released on the band’s YouTube channel in 2023.

COVID fundamentally changed the music scene, and the band never quite recovered. It is believed to have several albums’ worth of unreleased material, which the band plans to release when the time is right.
